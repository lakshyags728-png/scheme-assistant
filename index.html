Does any of this apply?

Rules are summarised from public scheme guidelines and were last reviewed on 22 November 2026. Benefits, income limits and application windows change, and state governments add their own conditions. This is guidance, not an official decision. Confirm every scheme on its official portal or at your nearest Common Service Centre before you apply.

Central schemes apply across India. State schemes are included only for Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh. Their rules were checked mainly against news and secondary sources because official portals could not be opened, and several are changing, so confirm on the state portal. myScheme.gov.in lets you search central and state schemes together.
